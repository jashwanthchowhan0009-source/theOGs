About theOG's

Made by the OGs, for the ones who stay original.

We're a small crew from Navi Mumbai who got tired of wearing what everyone else was wearing. theOG's is our answer: fewer pieces, made with more care.

We're starting with two tees, and we'll only add more when they're ready.

  1. Step 1

    Sketch

    Every design starts as a drawing, like the hand-painted flags in our gallery.

  2. Step 2

    Sample

    We test fabric, fit and print until the tee feels right.

  3. Step 3

    Print

    A small first run, checked piece by piece.

  4. Step 4

    Drop

    Released on a set date, announced first to people on our list.
